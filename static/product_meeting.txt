Meeting Title: Q2 Product Roadmap Discussion
Date: May 10, 2025
Participants: Sarah Chen, Michael Rodriguez, Alex Kim, Jordan Taylor, Priya Patel

[Sarah]: Good morning everyone. Thank you for joining our Q2 product roadmap discussion. Today we'll be focusing on three key areas: the new dashboard features, mobile app improvements, and the integration with third-party services.

[Michael]: Before we dive in, could we quickly review our progress on the Q1 deliverables? I think it will give us good context for planning Q2.

[Sarah]: Absolutely. In Q1, we completed the user authentication overhaul, launched the analytics dashboard beta, and released version 2.5 of the API. The only major item that slipped was the machine learning recommendation engine, which is now about 60% complete.

[Alex]: I can speak to that. We ran into some unexpected challenges with the data processing pipeline, but we've resolved those issues now. I'm confident we can complete it within the first three weeks of Q2.

[Jordan]: That's good to hear. The marketing team is eager to highlight those ML features in our next campaign, so having a firm timeline helps us plan.

[Priya]: From the UX research side, we've completed user testing on the new dashboard. The feedback has been mostly positive, but users are requesting better customization options and improved loading times.

[Sarah]: Thanks, Priya. Let's make those dashboard improvements our first priority for Q2. Michael, can you outline the technical work needed?

[Michael]: We need to refactor the component rendering to improve performance, implement the new customization API, and update the data fetching layer. I estimate it would take about 4 weeks with two engineers.

[Alex]: I can assign Wei and Sophia to this task. They're familiar with that part of the codebase.

[Sarah]: Perfect. Next on the agenda is the mobile app. Jordan, what are the key improvements we should focus on?

[Jordan]: Based on user feedback and app store reviews, the top three requests are: offline mode functionality, push notifications for important events, and improved navigation for larger phone screens.

[Priya]: I can confirm that. Our usability studies showed that users struggle with navigation on the mobile app, especially when using it one-handed.

[Michael]: For the offline mode, we'll need to implement a local database sync solution. That's going to be the most complex part.

[Sarah]: Let's prioritize the navigation improvements first since that affects all users, then tackle offline mode and push notifications. Alex, who can you assign to the mobile work?

[Alex]: I'll put Marcus and Leila on it. They led the last mobile update and have a good understanding of the architecture.

[Sarah]: Great. The last major item is third-party integrations. We've had many requests for better Salesforce and HubSpot integration. Priya, what are users looking for specifically?

[Priya]: They want bi-directional syncing of customer data, the ability to view our analytics within their CRM dashboards, and simplified authentication flows.

[Michael]: The authentication part is straightforward after our Q1 work. The bi-directional sync will require new API endpoints and a robust conflict resolution strategy.

[Jordan]: This would be a major selling point for our enterprise customers. I'd suggest prioritizing the Salesforce integration first since that's used by most of our larger accounts.

[Sarah]: Agreed. Let's schedule the Salesforce integration for the first half of Q2 and HubSpot for the second half.

[Alex]: I'll assign our integration specialists, Raj and Elena, to this project.

[Sarah]: Excellent. So our Q2 roadmap includes: dashboard improvements, mobile app updates prioritizing navigation, and third-party integrations starting with Salesforce. Are there any other items we should discuss?

[Michael]: We should allocate some time for technical debt and infrastructure improvements. Our test coverage has dropped below 80%, and we have several deprecated libraries that need updating.

[Sarah]: Good point. Let's allocate 20% of engineering time to technical debt this quarter. Alex, please work with the team leads to identify the highest priority items.

[Alex]: Will do. I'll have a technical debt inventory ready by the end of the week.

[Jordan]: From the product marketing side, we'll need documentation updates and demo videos for all these new features. I'll coordinate with the content team.

[Priya]: And I'll plan user testing sessions throughout the development cycle to ensure we're meeting user expectations.

[Sarah]: Perfect. This sounds like a solid plan for Q2. I'll summarize these decisions in our product roadmap document and share it with everyone by tomorrow. Thanks for your input, everyone!

[Meeting End]
